👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Research Engineer at Shanghai AI Laboratory, working on large language model post-training, mathematical reasoning, and AI agents. I contribute to the post-training of Intern-S1-Pro and Intern-S2-Preview, with a focus on mathematical reasoning data, SFT, and RLVR.

Previously, I worked with the OpenMMLab generative image and video team on diffusion models, image editing, and character animation. Before that, I was an applied researcher at Tencent, where I developed video understanding and efficient vision systems for large-scale products. I received both my M.S. and B.E. degrees from the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University.

My research interests include LLM post-training, AI agents, and generative visual models.

🚀 Projects

InternS1-MO: Olympiad-level Mathematics Agent

I developed an agent pipeline for CMO- and IMO-level problems, combining parallel proof generation, lemma-level voting, proof verification, first-error localization, and candidate selection. The agent provides users with an olympiad-level mathematical reasoning experience. Online Demo

Qian Qiu Shi Song: AIGC Animation Production

I developed generative visual algorithms for the CCTV animated series Qian Qiu Shi Song, supporting the creation of characters, scenes, and props. The production workflow combined SDXL LoRA training, image-to-image generation, local repainting, and style fine-tuning to maintain visual consistency across shots. The series was aired on CCTV-1. CCTV Page

OpenMMLab Generative AI Ecosystem

As a core contributor to MMagic, I integrated image and video generation methods including Stable Diffusion, ControlNet Animation, and AnimateDiff, and maintained training, inference, configuration, and documentation workflows. I also supported image generation, editing, and animation capabilities for MagicMaker / InternLM Magic.
