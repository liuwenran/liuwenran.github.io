👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Research Engineer at Shanghai AI Laboratory, working on large language model post-training, mathematical reasoning, and AI agents. I contribute to the post-training of Intern-S1-Pro and Intern-S2-Preview, with a focus on mathematical reasoning data, supervised fine-tuning, and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.

Previously, I worked with the OpenMMLab generative image and video team on diffusion models, image editing, and character animation. Before that, I was an applied researcher at Tencent, where I developed video understanding and efficient vision systems for large-scale products. I received both my M.S. and B.E. degrees from the Department of Automation at Tsinghua University.

My research interests include LLM post-training and reasoning, agentic AI and AI for research, and generative visual intelligence.

🔬 Research

LLM Post-training and Mathematical Reasoning

I build and curate mathematical reasoning data for supervised fine-tuning and RLVR, spanning basic computation, competition mathematics, and long-form proofs. I contributed to the mathematical reasoning capabilities of Intern-S1-Pro and Intern-S2-Preview.

Agentic AI and AI for Research

I develop agents that automate data synthesis, training, evaluation, and experimental analysis. My recent work includes the Researcher module in TREX, the SciExplore benchmark, and an olympiad-level mathematics agent deployed in the InternLM online platform.

Generative Visual Intelligence

I have worked on diffusion-based image generation and editing, human image animation, and controllable 4D character generation. Representative projects include PowerPaint, HumanVid, and CharacterShot. I also supported visual asset generation for the CCTV animated series Qian Qiu Shi Song.
